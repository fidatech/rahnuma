# Rahnuma Product Design System — V2

Rahnuma V2 deliberately avoids green as a brand color. Green is reserved for success/positive states only.

## Core palette

- Midnight Ink: `#101828` — navigation, premium institutional surfaces
- Cobalt: `#3157D5` — primary actions, links, focus and active states
- Deep Cobalt: `#2444AD` — hover/pressed states
- Copper: `#C97B4B` — selective premium accent
- Cloud: `#F3F5F8` — application background
- White: `#FFFFFF` — content surfaces
- Border: `#E4E7EC`
- Muted text: `#667085`
- Success only: `#17845B`
- Warning: `#C8841D`
- Danger: `#C2414B`

## Product personalities

### Marketplace
Editorial, spacious and property-first. Dark hero, strong search, premium property cards and agency attribution.

### Rahnama ERP
Dense enough for daily business use but not spreadsheet-like. Persistent operations navigation, KPI hierarchy, quick actions, structured forms and finance-focused tables.

### Regulatory workspace
Institutional and restrained. Uses the same product system but emphasizes compliance, Setta, inspections, licensing and violations without pretending to be an official Ministry product while `GOVERNMENT_MODE=false`.

### Mobile
Material 3 with the same midnight/cobalt/copper identity, structured dashboards and operational cards instead of generic ListTiles.
